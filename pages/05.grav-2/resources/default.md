# Resources

Links for going further with Grav 2, the free projects built on it, and Markdown itself.

## Grav 2

- [Grav](https://getgrav.org/) – the official site, with downloads, themes and plugins
- [Grav Learn](https://learn.getgrav.org/) – the documentation for Grav
- [Grav on GitHub](https://github.com/getgrav/grav) – the source code, and where to report issues
- [Grav forum](https://getgrav.org/forum) and [Grav Discord](https://chat.getgrav.org/) – the community, for questions and help
- From the Grav blog: [Grav 2.0 Released!](https://getgrav.org/blog/grav-2-stable-released) · [Admin 2.1: Every String on Your Site, Editable](https://getgrav.org/blog/grav-2-admin-2-1) · [Grav 2.1: Every Page Speaks Markdown](https://getgrav.org/blog/grav-2-1-released) · [Grav 2.2: 2x Faster](https://getgrav.org/blog/grav-2-2-released) · [Grav vs WordPress: An Honest Comparison](https://getgrav.org/blog/grav-vs-wordpress) · [99 Security Findings, Fixed and Released](https://getgrav.org/blog/oss-scanner-security-releases)

## Writing in Markdown

- [What Is Markdown, and How Do You Use It?](https://www.howtogeek.com/448323/what-is-markdown-and-how-do-you-use-it/) – a friendly introduction (How-To Geek)
- [The Markdown Guide](https://www.markdownguide.org/) – a free reference
- [Improving the Accessibility of Your Markdown](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2021/09/improving-accessibility-of-markdown/) – headings, alt text and links (Smashing Magazine)

## Other Options

- [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/) – publish a Markdown file as a web page instantly, with no hosting needed
- [Grav Helios Open Reader](https://hibbittsdesign.org/grav-helios-open-reader.html) – a premium counterpart to Open Publishing Space, for open textbooks and guides
- [Grav Helios Course Hub](https://hibbittsdesign.org/grav-helios-course-hub.html) – a premium counterpart to Open Course Hub, for open courses

Both are built on Helios, a paid theme sold by the Grav team at getgrav.org – its sales help fund Grav CMS development.
