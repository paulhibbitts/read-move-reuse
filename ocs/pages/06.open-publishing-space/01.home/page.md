# A DIY Path from Plain Markdown or Pressbooks

*Paul Hibbitts*

Open textbooks and guides are meant to be reused and remixed, but they often live inside a single platform – which can make them harder to adapt or self-host elsewhere, even for their own authors.

This short course introduces Open Publishing Space, a free, open-source website starter for web-first open textbooks and guides, alongside a blog and other pages – plus a free converter that brings in a Pressbooks book.

In three words: **share** pages designed for all kinds of readers, **move** your content wherever you go, and **reuse** your Markdown.

This version is a course site, built with Open Course Space, so the course navbar, module list and header image you're using are its features at work – the same Markdown, moved from [the original guide](https://share-move-reuse.hibbittsdesign.org/open-publishing-space?target=_blank), built with Open Publishing Space.

> [!TIP]
> **Built on Grav 2:** the new version of [Grav](https://getgrav.org/?target=_blank), a free, open-source content management system (CMS) – with a modern Admin 2 panel rebuilt from scratch, a Markdown version of every page and faster loading pages, still just files with no database.
