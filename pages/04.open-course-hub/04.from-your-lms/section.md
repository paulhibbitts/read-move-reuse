Already have a course in your LMS? Start from it, with a free converter (in beta) – no Grav installation needed to see what carries over:

- **[Common Cartridge Converter](https://common-cartridge-converter.hibbittsdesign.org/)** – export a course from Canvas, Moodle, Brightspace or Blackboard, and download its modules, ready for Open Course Hub

Example (a raw conversion, with no revisions or fine-tuning): [an LMS course in Open Course Hub](https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/grav-open-course-hub-common-cartridge-it2dda/)

Already in Docsify? The [Open Publishing Course Hub Converter](https://open-publishing-course-hub-converter.hibbittsdesign.org/) moves a course between Docsify and Open Course Hub, in either direction – see [a course on Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/?basePath=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-this-open-course-hub-site/main&homepage=home.md&sidebar=true&loadSidebar=_sidebar.md&loadNavbar=_navbar.md&loadFooter=_footer.md&pagination=true&searchbox=true&name=CPT-363).
