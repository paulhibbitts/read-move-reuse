All the starters and converters in this guide are **free and open-source**:

- **"I want to share an open textbook or guide"** → Open Publishing Space 2: demo with [one guide](https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/grav-open-publishing-quark2/) or [several guides](https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/grav-open-publishing-quark2-guides/) · [download](https://getgrav.org/downloads/skeletons#open-publishing-space-site)
- **"I want an open home for my course"** → Open Course Hub 2: [demo](https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/grav-open-course-hub/) · [download](https://getgrav.org/downloads/skeletons#course-hub-site)
- **"I already have a Pressbooks book, an LMS course or Markdown in Docsify-This"** → the [Pressbooks](https://pressbooks-converter.hibbittsdesign.org/), [Common Cartridge](https://common-cartridge-converter.hibbittsdesign.org/) or [Docsify](https://open-publishing-course-hub-converter.hibbittsdesign.org/) Converter, to make it the base of a new site

Both starters are edited in the browser with Grav's Admin 2 panel, or as Markdown files with any editor or via Git.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Good to know:** publishing as a website keeps things simple, but there's no one-click ebook (EPUB or formatted PDF – pages print cleanly from the browser), you'll need a PHP web host (PHP 8.3 or newer, no database), colleagues new to Markdown may need a little help to start, and Grav extras like definition and objectives boxes are shortcodes that other tools would need converted.

With thanks to the authors of the openly licensed book and course used as examples, for sharing their work in the open 🙏🏻
