Many platforms can publish openly licensed textbooks, guides and courses – but direct access to the content itself, for readers and authors alike, is often not as easy. This short guide introduces [Grav 2](https://getgrav.org/?target=_blank), a free, open-source platform that keeps every page as a plain Markdown file on a site you control – editable in the browser, with the Markdown behind any page a click away.

You'll see why it suits open education and publishing, the free starter skeletons and converters built on it, and how to explore its demos and Admin 2 with nothing to install. This guide is one of [five on this site](/guides), itself built with Grav 2 and Open Publishing Space.
