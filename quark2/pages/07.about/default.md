# About This Site

This site holds five short guides to free, open-source DIY tools for open textbooks, guides and courses:

- **[Open Textbooks, Guides and Courses](/guide)** – both website starters, plus the converters that bring in a Pressbooks book or LMS course
- **[Open Textbooks and Guides](/open-publishing-space)** – Open Publishing Space on its own, with the Pressbooks Converter (originally prepared for the ETUG Fall Workshop 2026)
- **[Open Courses](/open-course-hub)** – Open Course Hub and Open MultiCourse Hub, with the Common Cartridge Converter
- **[Grav 2 for Open Education and Publishing](/grav-2)** – Grav 2 itself, and the free starters, plugins and converters built on it
- **[Docsify for Open Education and Publishing](/docsify)** – Docsify-This and the Docsify Starter Kits, for Markdown on the web with no web server to set up, plus a path to Grav

The site itself is built with Open Publishing Space, so it shows a few of its features at work:

- **Several guides on one site**, each with a card on the [Guides](/) page
- **Pages for each guide** – a guide's own Topics and Resources join the menu while you read it, after a divider
- **Site pages alongside the guides**, like this one

**Read, Move, Reuse:** pages designed for every reader · your content goes wherever you go · your Markdown is always yours to reuse – and anyone's, with an open licence.
